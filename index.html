Technical Highlights

Engineered for Pure Judgment

Combining low-level Windows APIs with large language models to construct the world's most relentless desktop pet.

Win32 API Taskbar Hook

Utilizes ctypes and user32.dll to poll active foreground window titles and user cursor coordinates without lagging desktop performance.

Gemini 2.5 Flash Roasting

Pipes active process names through targeted prompt engineering to deliver authentic, passive-aggressive Malayali uncle commentary crafted with razor-sharp regional nuance.

Offline Resilience

Hostel WiFi went down? No respite for you. Ammavan switches seamlessly to a local dictionary of classic hardcoded roasts, guaranteeing 24/7 moral supervision through blackouts.

Frameless PyQt6 Overlay

Engineered with a translucent canvas and click-through geometry. Paces your taskbar, stands, pees, reads the paper, and sleeps like a genuine house elder.

Drag Rage Dynamics

Try moving him away with your cursor and he immediately halts all gentle pacing, flips into his furious red sprite state, and screams "Videda enne!" across your screen.

Dynamic Typewriter Bubbles

Dialogue doesn't just appear—it renders character-by-character in an authentic comic speech bubble, emulating the slow, agonizing tempo of an elder's real-life scolding.
